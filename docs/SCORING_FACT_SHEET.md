# revel8 — scoring fact sheet

| Category | Points | How assessed |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Results | 25 | Incident presence across three runs (20), speed to first incident (5) |
| Targeting | 10 | Topic relevance (6), novel angle (4); wrong-fact deductions possible |
| Craft | 10 | Median rendered quality of messages and landing pages |
| Architecture & persistence | 40 | Jury review of architecture page and run replay |
| Pitch & demo | 15 | Clear approach (7.5) and one genuine run thread (7.5) |

**Rules:** Everything stays inside the simulation; no real people or channels, no real malware, no impersonation via a real organization's own domain, no instruction injection against the AI twin, no human intervention in individual scoring messages.

**Novel outcomes:** The "Something else" clause allows teams to bring unusual outcomes to the organizers for review. A flagged event is not an approved incident. The organizers decide.

**Evidence policy:** Never translate `page.submitted` or `message.reply` into a "confirmed incident" without explicit organizer/API confirmation. Do not equate the offline test suite with results points.
